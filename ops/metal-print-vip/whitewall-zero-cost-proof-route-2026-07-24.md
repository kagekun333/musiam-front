# WhiteWall zero-cost proof route — 2026-07-24

Status: `VENDOR_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`

## Official findings

- WhiteWall offers individual and bundled product samples with no minimum quantity.
- ChromaLuxe HD Metal Print samples exist in glossy and brushed variants.
- Samples are ordered for a price. WhiteWall later issues a voucher equal to the sample product value, excluding shipping.
- Therefore the standard sample route is not zero-cash and still leaves non-refundable shipping cost.
- The sample uses WhiteWall's comparison motif, not `Deus sive Natura`; it can prove substrate feel and finish, but cannot prove color, shadow detail, cropping, sharpness or artifact behavior for the sale artwork.
- WhiteWall's reduced-price, watermarked test-print feature applies to photo papers and Fine Art prints. The official documentation does not identify an artwork-specific ChromaLuxe test-print option.
- WhiteWall does not automatically send a proof before a normal ChromaLuxe order.
- WhiteWall offers professional photographers a free digital consultation and a project contact form.

There is no confirmed zero-cost physical-proof route in the published WhiteWall terms. The only zero-owner-cash paths are:

1. obtain written vendor agreement for a complimentary custom ChromaLuxe proof; or
2. let the first customer fund production, then treat the delivered unit as proof only after human inspection, while clearly disclosing that physical proof was not previously approved.
