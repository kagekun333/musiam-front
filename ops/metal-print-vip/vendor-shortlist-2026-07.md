# Print-proof vendor shortlist — July 2026

This is a research shortlist for quotation and proof comparison. It is **not** an order, vendor selection, or production authorization.

## Recommended first quotation: Metal Print Japan

- Official site: <https://www.metal-print.jp/>
- Why it is first: the provider describes its product as a ChromaLuxe aluminium high-end photo panel, accepts orders from one print, and describes an order flow that includes image submission, price confirmation, payment, then production/shipping. Its stated normal shipping window is 3–10 business days.
- Technical check: its FAQ gives 300dpi as a recommended reference and says a 3,024 × 4,032px image supports a 200 × 290mm panel at that reference. **Inference:** the four 3000 × 3000px square candidates are appropriate to ask about for a roughly 10-inch-square proof. The printer must confirm the final size, crop, colour profile, and proof result.
- Ask first: a four-work proof proposal, 3-copy-per-work quotation, finish/substrate options, colour-management instructions, packaging, and damage/reprint handling.

## Comparison quotation: Metalia

- Official site: <https://www.metalia.jp/>
- Why compare: it also presents ChromaLuxe metal panels as a high-value photo/interior product and publicly lists an 8 × 10-inch product size. Its site states that image preparation can include noise and blur handling; the actual source must nevertheless remain unaltered unless explicitly approved.
- Ask first: whether it can provide an artist-edition proof workflow, exact material/finish, file-handling policy, and a comparable three-copy quotation.

## Production selection rule

Do not select a vendor only on base print price. The chosen quote must lock all of the following:

1. Final square dimensions and effective resolution for the approved proof.
2. Substrate, coating, mount/hanging hardware, and packaging.
3. Colour profile and a proof-approval process.
4. Unit price, proof cost, total cost for 12 units, taxes, and shipping.
5. Production and replacement lead time.
6. A written policy for damaged deliveries, reprints, and cancellation.
