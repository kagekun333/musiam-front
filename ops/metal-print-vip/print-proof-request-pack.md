## Quote requested

1. A metal-print substrate and finish recommendation for a 3000px-square source.
2. Recommended square physical size(s), including the effective print resolution.
3. Proof method, proof cost, colour-management requirements, and proof lead time.
4. Unit cost at three copies per artwork; packaging, domestic/international shipping, taxes, and reprint/damage handling as separate line items.
5. Production lead time after proof approval, serial-numbering options, and signing/framing options.
6. File-delivery method that preserves source ownership and avoids public exposure.

Start with the comparison candidates recorded in `vendor-shortlist-2026-07.md`; do not send this pack or upload any source file until the operator selects a vendor and explicitly approves the proof charge.

## Required response format

- Quote validity period

- Exact substrate, coating, mount, dimensions, weight, and hanging hardware
- Proof acceptance criteria and colour profile
- Production, fulfilment, replacement, and cancellation terms
